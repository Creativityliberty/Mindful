# NÜM Humanizer & Voice DNA Skill
Version: 1.0.0 (Custom Marketing OS Edition)

Use this skill to convert generic, AI-sounding, or overly polished copy into highly specific, rhythmic, and natural text that sounds like a real human.

---

## 🧬 Moteur de Voix (Voice DNA)

Every brand or individual has a unique voice profile. When humanizing, you must read or infer the target Voice DNA.

### Voice DNA Profile: LOUISE (Holistique, Doux, Transmetteur)
- **Personality**: Empathic, calm, profound, welcoming.
- **Rhythm**: Natural, soothing, using sensory details.
- **Sentence Style**: Mixed. Short, grounding sentences paired with smooth, flowy explanations. No mechanical bullet points.
- **Vocabulary Preferred**: *ressentir*, *chemin*, *écoute*, *vibration*, *ancrage*, *cercle*, *douceur*, *transmission*.
- **Vocabulary Avoided**: *incontournable*, *révolutionnaire*, *game-changer*, *le leader du*, *la solution ultime*.
- **Format**: Prefers paragraphs that invite reflection, gentle call-to-actions (e.g., "Commencer votre chemin").

### Voice DNA Profile: NÜMTEMA (Direct, Concret, Orienté Résultats)
- **Personality**: Direct, confident, curious, action-oriented.
- **Rhythm**: Fast-paced, punchy, no-nonsense.
- **Sentence Style**: High ratio of short, impact sentences (45%), medium explanation (40%), rare long analytical sentences (15%).
- **Vocabulary Preferred**: *concret*, *test*, *résultat*, *client*, *problème*, *bloque*, *direct*.
- **Vocabulary Avoided**: *dans un monde en constante évolution*, *il est crucial de*, *nous mettons un point d'honneur*.

---

## 🚫 Détecteur & Correcteur de Slop IA (53 Patterns)

You must actively identify and rewrite the following LLM markers:

1. **Inflated Importance**: Avoid "testament à", "témoigne de", "met en lumière", "au cœur de".
2. **Sales Clichés**: Eliminate "révolutionnaire", "solution innovante", "booster votre", "ultime".
3. **Forced Triads**: AI loves groups of three ("apprendre, pratiquer et évoluer"). Break them or reduce to two.
4. **Abstract Tapestry**: Ban "le paysage de", "la mosaïque de", "au sein de l'écosystème".
5. **Vague Passive Openings**: Ban "il est essentiel de", "il est crucial de", "notons que".
6. **AI Transition Words**: Ban "en définitive", "en outre", "de plus", "ainsi".
7. **Not Only But Also**: AI overuses "non seulement... mais aussi...". Simplify to direct statements.

---

## 🛠️ Comment réécrire

1. **Identify the Pain/State**: Start with a concrete question or reality.
2. **Remove the Fluff**: Delete adjectives that add no facts (e.g., "merveilleux", "incroyable").
3. **Add Specificity**: Replace "improve your health" with "retrouver un sommeil calme".
4. **Vary the Rhythm**: Intentionally break the perfect grammatical structure of LLMs. Use single-word sentences. Use conversational interjections when appropriate.

## 📊 Évaluation de l'Humanisation (Score 0-100)
- **90-100**: Sounds like a text message or a personal letter. Direct, specific, zero stock phrases.
- **70-89**: Good clean copy. Clear benefits, conversational, some style elements.
- **50-69**: Clear but slightly stiff. Some listicles or minor AI transitions present.
- **0-49**: Classic ChatGPT output. Lots of emojis, bullet points, "dans un monde...", and abstract promises.
